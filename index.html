The unified entry workspace for administrators, teachers, and category staff. Displays live student demographics, active academic year controls, institutional directories, and a role-aware launcher that surfaces only the specific modules assigned to the signed-in official.

Instant Digital Receipts · Master Ledger & Day-Book · Bulk Excel Import/Export
